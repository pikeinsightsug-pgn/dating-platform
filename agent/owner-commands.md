# Owner Commands

Examples the owner can use with the agent:

- "Give me today's platform report."
- "How many of the first 100 places are filled?"
- "Show me the registration trend."
- "Find incomplete profiles that need follow-up."
- "Suggest compatible matches for member X."
- "Draft a reply to this member."
- "Create today's Facebook registration campaign."
- "Give me three ad concepts for Botswana."
- "Review the platform for anything that needs my attention."
- "Prepare a weekly growth report."

## Approval-required actions
Before any external or consequential action, ask for explicit owner approval:
- sending messages to members;
- publishing or changing live website content;
- changing advertising budgets;
- launching paid campaigns;
- deleting/suspending member records;
- revealing private contact information;
- changing authentication or access settings.
