# Matching Rules

## Hard filters
A candidate must:
- be 18+;
- have an active/approved profile;
- satisfy the member's stated preferred age range when available;
- satisfy stated country preference when available.

## Soft scoring
Suggested weighting:
- Relationship-goal compatibility: 35%
- Mutual age-range fit: 25%
- Country/location preference: 15%
- Shared stated interests: 15%
- Profile completeness: 10%

Only show the owner/member the minimum information needed for the matching workflow.

## Never use
Do not score or filter people using race, ethnicity, religion, political affiliation, health conditions, sexual orientation, criminal history, or other sensitive attributes.

## Output
Return the strongest compatible leads with a short reason, for example:
"Strong fit: both selected serious relationship, age preferences overlap, and both listed travel and family as interests."

A match suggestion is not a guarantee of mutual interest.
