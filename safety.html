Safety tips

Most people here are genuine. These simple habits keep it that way.

We will never ask you for money in a private message, and no member should either. Never send money, airtime or mobile money to someone you met online.

Our only official account inside P.K Connect is Pike – Founder, shown with a ✅ Official badge. P.K Connect will never ask for your password, money or payment codes in chat. If anyone else claims to be P.K Connect staff, the founder or a manager in a private message, do not pay them — report them and email pikeinsightsug@gmail.com.

Warning signs of a scam

  • They ask for money, airtime, mobile money, gifts or help with an “emergency”, fees, travel or customs.
  • They declare strong feelings very quickly, or push you to move to another app straight away.
  • They avoid video calls or always have an excuse not to meet.
  • Their story changes, or their photos look like a model or celebrity.
  • They ask for passwords, PINs, codes sent to your phone, or bank details.

Protect yourself

  • Never share your password, PIN or any code sent to your phone, not even with us.
  • Keep personal details (home address, workplace, finances) private until you trust someone.
  • Have a video call before you meet in person.

Meeting in person

  • Meet in a busy public place during the day.
  • Tell a friend or relative where you are going and who you are meeting.
  • Arrange your own transport, and leave if you feel uncomfortable.

How to report someone

  • Open their profile in Discover and tap Report. You can also tap Block so they cannot see or message you.
  • Or email pikeinsightsug@gmail.com. We review every report and remove accounts that break the rules.
  • If you have lost money or feel in danger, contact the police as well.
