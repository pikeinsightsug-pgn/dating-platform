Privacy notice

Last updated 18 October 2026 · Questions: pikeinsightsug@gmail.com

PikeConnection is a dating community for adults aged 18 and over in Uganda and Botswana. This notice explains what we collect, why, and who can see it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password (stored securely by our authentication provider; we never see your password).
  • Profile answers: display name, age, gender, country, city or area, who you would like to meet, your relationship goal, a short biography, and your preferred partner age range.
  • Optional: a profile photo, interests and partner qualities you choose to add later.
  • Messages you send to other members, and any blocks or reports you make.

We do not ask for your ID number, home address, financial details or payment card. No payment is requested during the current testing phase.

Who can see your information

  • Before approval your profile is visible only to you and to the platform owner, who reviews it.
  • After approval other signed-in, approved members can see your display name, age, gender, country, city, relationship goal, biography, interests and photo.
  • Never shown to members: your email address, your preferred partner age range, your partner qualities, and your account details.
  • Messages can be read only by the two members in the conversation. If you block someone, you stop seeing each other.
  • Visitors who are not signed in cannot see any member profile or photo.

Photos

Photos are stored in private storage, not on a public web page. They are shown only to you, to the platform owner for review, and to signed-in approved members. Please upload only photos of yourself that you are happy for other members to see. You can replace your photo at any time from your profile.

How we use your information

  • To create your account, review your profile and show it to other approved members.
  • To keep the community safe: checking that members are adults, investigating reports, and removing fake or abusive profiles.
  • To contact you about your account by email.

We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Accounts, profiles, messages and photos are stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile at any time after logging in.
  • Ask us to hide your profile, correct your information or delete your account by emailing pikeinsightsug@gmail.com from the address you registered with.

Earlier registrations

If you registered through our earlier Google Form, your answers are kept privately by the platform owner. They are not shown to other members. They are linked to your new account only after we confirm with you that it is yours.
